## Verification Notes

**Primary sources only.** All URLs listed above are primary sources — either the project's own website/repository, or the original publication of a cited paper. No third-party claims (blog posts about a project, reviews, news articles) are used as sole evidence for any factual assertion.

**Verification method:** Each source was selected because it is the authoritative origin of the claim it supports. For contemporary projects (Omarchy, Brain, Open Interpreter, OpenDAN), the project's own site or repository is the definitive source for what the project does and claims. For historical references (Licklider, Kay & Goldberg), the original publications are cited.

**Claim boundaries:** This project's novel claims (the synthesis argument, the conceptual ladder, the architectural distinctiveness of Level 3) are analytical contributions that cite prior art for context, not for authority. The claim "no existing project combines all seven dimensions" is an empirical observation based on examination of each project's stated capabilities and documentation.

**Living document:** As the project develops and new sources are incorporated, each must be added here with its verification status before being cited elsewhere in the project.
